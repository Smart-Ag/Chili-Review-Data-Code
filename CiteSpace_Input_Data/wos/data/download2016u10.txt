Reducing end-effector dimensions and widening stem spacing are promising research directions because they significantly improved goal configuration success, from 63% to 84%. However, the fruit location at the stem is the strongest influencing parameter and therefore provides an incentive to train or breed plants that develop more fruit at the front side of the plant stem. The two analyses may serve as useful tools to study motion planning problems in a dense obstacle environment. (C) 2015 IAgrE. Published by Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.
