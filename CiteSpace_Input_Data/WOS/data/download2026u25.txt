Comparative experiments verified that the proposed method outperformed conventional load identification methods, and its successful implementation on embedded hardware demonstrated its applicability for on-machine real-time load identification. Overall, this method provides a robust and physically interpretable solution for pepper harvesting drum load identification and supports the optimization of pepper harvester operation.
